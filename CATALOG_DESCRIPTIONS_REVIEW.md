## FPS-RIGID-01 — Rigid Base Anchor
**DRAFT:**
A heavy steel baseplate welded or bolted flat to structural steel or concrete, with a vertical riser clamp or lug welded directly to its face that grips the pipe without any articulation. The assembly is visually distinguished by its solid, fixed profile — no springs, pivots, or sliding components are present. Two or four anchor bolt holes are typically visible at the corners of the baseplate, and the pipe clamp is secured with hex-head bolts on both sides of the pipe barrel.

## FPS-SPRING-02 — Variable Spring Hanger
**DRAFT:**
A cylindrical steel canister, typically painted with a load-scale indicator window on the side, suspended from a structural attachment point above and connected to the pipe below via a threaded rod and pipe clamp. Inside the canister a coil spring is visible through the indicator slot or scale plate, and a travel indicator pin or arrow shows the current compressed position against a graduated scale painted on the housing. The unit hangs vertically, with a clevis or eye-nut at the top and a turnbuckle or swivel connection at the bottom linking to the pipe saddle or U-bolt clamp.

## FPS-SHOCK-03 — Hydraulic Shock Snubber
**DRAFT:**
A cylindrical hydraulic cylinder body, usually silver or bare-metal finish, mounted in-line between a structural bracket and a pipe lug or trunnion clamp, oriented at an angle or horizontally depending on the installation. Both ends terminate in clevis pin connections — visible forked brackets with a through-bolt — allowing the unit to pivot slightly in its plane of motion. The cylinder body is smooth with no external spring coils, and a small hydraulic fluid reservoir or bleed port fitting is sometimes visible near one end cap.
